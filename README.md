*Discreption: The E-Learning Platform UI is designed to provide students with a seamless and engaging learning experience. It includes multiple pages and sections that cover all the essential features of a real-world e-learning application, including course discovery, progress tracking, video-based learning, and achievement recognition.
The home page serves as the landing page of the platform. It features a visually striking hero section with an animated gradient background, a bold headline, and clear call-to-action buttons that guide users to either start learning or view their progress. The page also displays key statistics such as total students, number of courses available, satisfaction rate, and average rating. A Popular Courses section showcases featured courses with thumbnail images, ratings, difficulty levels, and lesson counts. Additionally, a Browse by Topic section allows users to explore courses by category such as Web Development, Data Science, UI/UX Design, AI and Machine Learning, Mobile Development, and Cybersecurity.
The Explore page provides a comprehensive listing of all available courses on the platform. It includes a fully functional search bar that allows users to search for courses by title, author, or topic in real time. Filter tabs are provided at the top to sort courses by category. Each course card displays the course thumbnail, title, author name, rating, number of lessons, estimated duration, and difficulty level. Users can click on any course card to open the video player and begin learning immediately.
The Dashboard is the heart of the platform's progress tracking system. It features a personalized greeting, a sidebar with navigation options, and key statistics displayed through progress cards including total courses enrolled, lessons completed, and total hours learned. A weekly activity bar chart visually represents the student's daily learning activity. The sidebar also includes a learning streak tracker that shows how many consecutive days the student has been learning, along with a weekly goal progress bar. This section is specifically designed to motivate students and help them stay consistent with their learning journey.
The My Courses tab within the Dashboard displays all enrolled courses with detailed progress information. Each course shows the completion percentage, number of lessons completed out of total, and a color-coded status chip indicating whether the course is In Progress, Not Started, or Completed. A Resume or Start button is also provided for quick access to continue learning from where the student left off.
The Video Player page simulates a real-world video learning experience. It includes a custom-designed video player with controls for play and pause, seek, next and previous lesson, volume, and fullscreen. A progress bar shows how far the student has watched the current lesson. On the right side, a full course playlist is displayed with all sections and lessons, clearly showing which lessons are completed, which is currently active, and which are remaining. Below the video player, tabs are provided for Overview, Notes, Q&A, and Resources, making it a complete and immersive learning environment within a single page.
The Achievements section displays badges earned by the student based on their learning milestones. Badges such as Fast Learner, On Fire, Goal Crusher, and Note Taker are awarded for various accomplishments. Locked badges are also shown to encourage students to keep learning and unlock new rewards.
